# Code of Conduct

We are building infrastructure for people who are routinely excluded from powerful networks. Participation must be respectful, specific, and safe.

Harassment, discrimination, doxxing, unwanted solicitation, intimidation, and publication of private founder or contact data are unacceptable. Debate ideas and evidence without attacking people. Maintainers may remove content or participation that threatens community safety or the project’s responsible-data principles.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer channel configured by the repository owner. Reports will be handled confidentially to the extent possible.
